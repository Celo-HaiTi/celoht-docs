# Research & Publications

## Purpose

CeloHT supports independent research into its model and outcomes, and publishes its own methodology and findings openly — treating research scrutiny as a trust-building mechanism, not a risk to manage.

## Research Partnerships

Universities and research institutions may partner with CeloHT to study financial inclusion outcomes, Agent Network dynamics, or reforestation methodology — see [PARTNERS.md](./PARTNERS.md#partner-categories). CeloHT provides access to aggregate, de-identified data consistent with [PRIVACY.md](./PRIVACY.md).

## Data Access for Researchers

Researchers can request access to aggregate program data beyond what's in the public API (see [API_REFERENCE.md](./API_REFERENCE.md)) by contacting contact@celoht.com with a research proposal. Requests are reviewed by the Governance & Legal Working Group for privacy compliance before approval.
