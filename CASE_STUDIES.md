# Case Studies

## Purpose

This document explains how CeloHT documents and publishes case studies — the standard and process, since specific published case studies will grow over time as programs mature (consistent with the incremental-by-nature note in [AUTHORS.md](./AUTHORS.md#note-on-this-list)).

## What Makes a Valid CeloHT Case Study

1. **Verifiable** — references specific, dated program activity (a training cohort, a planting event, an agent onboarding cluster), not general claims
2. **Honest about limitations** — notes what wasn't measured or what's uncertain, consistent with [IMPACT.md](./IMPACT.md#what-celoht-does-not-claim)
3. **Attributable** — names the community, region, or program (with consent), not anonymized in a way that prevents verification
4. **Methodologically consistent** — uses the same metric definitions as [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md), not bespoke favorable framing

## Case Study Template

```markdown
# Case Study: [Program/Region/Cohort Name]

## Context
[What community, what timeframe, what starting conditions]

## Intervention
[Which pillar(s), what specifically was done]

## Outcomes
[What changed, with specific numbers tied to METRICS.md definitions]

## Methodology Notes
[How outcomes were measured, what wasn't measured, confidence level]

## Community Voice
[Direct input from community members/agents involved, with consent]
```

## Publication Process

Draft case studies go through Working Group review (relevant pillar) and a fact-check pass against [METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) before publication, per [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Where Case Studies Are Published

Published case studies live in this repository under a `case-studies/` directory (added as the first verified case study is ready for publication) and are cross-linked from [IMPACT.md](./IMPACT.md) and the website's impact section.
